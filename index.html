How these differ from the general set

These are independent copies, not the shared pages with a parameter on the end. Editing one of them changes nothing anywhere else.

Dedicated, not switched

Each page declares data-speaker-dedicated, so Wilcox shows and the message-match copy reads as a real arrival even when the URL carries no ?s=. Ad platforms strip parameters more often than you would like.

Attribution survives anyway

Every CTA goes to register.bomal.org carrying whatever UTMs arrived, and falls back to utm_content=wilcox when the ad platform sends none. His registrations stay countable.

Same urgency switch

One date in assets/lp.js drives the top strip and every price on both pages. Before October 10 it counts down the early bird. After, it flips itself to the event date and the add-on tickets.

Notes before you start cutting

Page B now leads with a Wilcox quote, supplied by Brian, about nothing touching the Book of Mormon. Page A still has none. A line in his own voice is the strongest upgrade available to A as well.

Still no written social proof. No attendee quotes, no attendance number, no count of years running. For traffic that only knows Wilcox, that is the biggest remaining gap.
